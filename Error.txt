因為Comment是Oracle系統保留字，故會出現錯誤
ORA-00923: 在應出現的位置找不到 FROM 關鍵字

SELECT a.IP            AS IP,
       a.AREA          AS Area,
       a.MONITORSYSTEM AS SystemName,
       a.COMMENTS      AS Comment
  FROM TBL_MONITORPC_SYSTEMVERSION_IP a
 WHERE a.DELETEFLAG = 'N'
   AND a.AREA = :Area
   AND a.MONITORSYSTEM = :SystemName
 ORDER BY a.IP
